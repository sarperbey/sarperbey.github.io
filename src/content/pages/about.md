I'm Sarper. This site is where I keep what I read: links I saved with a short note on why they stuck, and now and then a longer post or a few images.

Most of it falls somewhere between history, politics, science and society. The notes are mine. The articles belong to their authors, so every entry links back to the source.

I want to write here in English, Italian and Turkish. For now it is mostly English.

You can follow along with the [RSS feed](/rss.xml), or find me through the links in the footer.
